## Foundation
- [ ] EG-1 | Be active in your troop for at least 6 months as a Life Scout. | Time
- [ ] EG-2 | Demonstrate Scout spirit by living the Scout Oath and Law. | Spirit

## Merit Badges
- [ ] EG-3 | Earn a total of 21 merit badges, including all 14 Eagle-required badges (list maintained in merit-badges.md). | Merit Badges

## Leadership
- [ ] EG-4 | While a Life Scout, serve actively for 6 months in a position of responsibility. | Leadership

## Eagle Project
- [ ] EG-5a | Develop a project idea and meet with your unit leader, committee, and district representative for approvals. | Project
- [ ] EG-5b | Plan the project in the Eagle Scout Service Project Workbook. | Project
- [ ] EG-5c | Get final approvals and carry out the project. | Project
- [ ] EG-5d | Complete the project report and workbook documentation. | Project

## Wrap-up
- [ ] EG-6 | Participate in a Scoutmaster conference. | Conference
- [ ] EG-7 | Successfully complete an Eagle Scout board of review. | Board

## Notes
- Eagle-required merit badges (14): Camping, Citizenship in Community, Citizenship in Nation, Citizenship in Society, Citizenship in World, Communication, Cooking, Cycling OR Hiking OR Swimming, Emergency Preparedness OR Lifesaving, Environmental Science OR Sustainability, Family Life, First Aid, Personal Fitness, Personal Management.
