## Hiking & Navigation
- [ ] 2C-1a | Demonstrate how a compass works and how to orient a map. Point out and identify map symbols and terrain features. | Navigation
- [ ] 2C-1b | On a troop activity, use a map and compass to follow a route and get from point A to B. | Navigation
- [ ] 2C-2a | Since joining, participate in five separate troop/patrol activities, at least three held outdoors (two overnight). | Outdoors
- [ ] 2C-2b | On one activity, plan/prep a meal and cook it outdoors. | Cooking
- [ ] 2C-2c | On one activity, demonstrate proper care, storage, and packing of gear. | Outdoors
- [ ] 2C-2d | On one activity, demonstrate ax, knife, and saw use and safety (Totin' Chip). | Tools

## Cooking & Fire
- [ ] 2C-3a | Since joining, participate in one outdoor cookout or campfire program. | Outdoors
- [ ] 2C-3b | Use the menu planned in 2C-2b to cook the meal; demonstrate fire-building or stove use. | Cooking
- [ ] 2C-3c | Serve as your patrol's cook for one meal. Supervise cleanup. | Cooking

## Tools & Knots
- [ ] 2C-4 | Use a pocketknife, ax, and saw safely. Earn Totin' Chip. | Tools
- [ ] 2C-5a | Demonstrate how to light a fire (or stove) in the field, with attention to safety and Leave No Trace. | Fire
- [ ] 2C-5b | Explain when fires are appropriate and LNT considerations. | Fire

## First Aid
- [ ] 2C-6a | Show first aid for: object in eye, bite of warm-blooded animal, puncture wounds, serious burns, heatstroke, shock, dehydration, hypothermia, hyperventilation. | First Aid
- [ ] 2C-6b | Show what to do for "hurry cases": stopped breathing, choking, severe bleeding, poisoning. | First Aid
- [ ] 2C-6c | Tell the five most common signs of a heart attack; explain steps for prevention. | First Aid
- [ ] 2C-6d | Demonstrate bandaging for injuries to head, upper arm, forearm, sprained ankle. | First Aid
- [ ] 2C-6e | Assemble a personal first-aid kit. Explain each item. | First Aid

## Swimming & Water Safety
- [ ] 2C-7a | Swim 100 yards using a resting stroke and a rescue stroke, with a strong stroke/side stroke. | Aquatics
- [ ] 2C-7b | Demonstrate water rescue methods: reach, throw, row, go with support. | Aquatics
- [ ] 2C-7c | Explain and demonstrate line rescue using a throw line. | Aquatics

## Citizenship, Fitness, Service
- [ ] 2C-8a | Participate in a flag ceremony. | Citizenship
- [ ] 2C-8b | Participate in an approved (minimum one hour) service project. | Service
- [ ] 2C-8c | Identify people in your community who could help if needed. | Citizenship
- [ ] 2C-9a | Explain the three R's of personal safety and protection. | Safety
- [ ] 2C-9b | Earn the Cyber Chip (or requalification) for your grade. | Safety
- [ ] 2C-10a | Record your fitness; plan 30 days of activity. | Fitness
- [ ] 2C-10b | Retest after 30 days: pushups, situps, sit-and-reach, 1-mile. Show improvement. | Fitness
- [ ] 2C-11 | Complete a Scoutmaster conference. | Conference
- [ ] 2C-12 | Complete a board of review. | Board
