## Foundation
- [ ] LF-1 | Be active in your troop for at least 6 months as a Star Scout. | Time
- [ ] LF-2 | Demonstrate Scout spirit by living the Scout Oath and Law. | Spirit

## Merit Badges
- [ ] LF-3 | Earn 5 more merit badges (3 from the Eagle-required list), beyond those earned for Star. | Merit Badges

## Service & Leadership
- [ ] LF-4 | Complete a service project totaling at least 6 hours of work. | Service
- [ ] LF-5 | While a Star Scout, serve actively for 6 months in a position of responsibility. | Leadership
- [ ] LF-6 | Participate in a Scoutmaster conference. | Conference
- [ ] LF-7 | Complete a board of review. | Board
