## Hiking & Navigation
- [ ] 1C-1a | Since joining, participate in 10 separate troop/patrol activities, six outdoors (three overnight). | Outdoors
- [ ] 1C-1b | On one outing, demonstrate the patrol method in action. | Patrol
- [ ] 1C-2a | Use a GPS unit (or phone app) to navigate a route on an outing. | Navigation
- [ ] 1C-2b | Since joining, complete a 5-mile hike using map and compass. | Outdoors
- [ ] 1C-3a | Since joining, camp overnight and prepare/cook a full meal for your patrol. | Cooking
- [ ] 1C-3b | Demonstrate proper food storage and camp sanitation on the outing. | Cooking

## Cooking & Menus
- [ ] 1C-4a | Plan a menu for a 3-day camping trip (5 people): include breakfast, lunch, dinner, snacks. | Cooking
- [ ] 1C-4b | Create a shopping list and food budget for the menu. | Cooking
- [ ] 1C-4c | Show how to transport, store, and prepare foods for camp. | Cooking
- [ ] 1C-4d | On the trip, cook two of the meals; serve as cook for one. Supervise cleanup. | Cooking

## Tools, Knots & Lashing
- [ ] 1C-5a | Demonstrate lashings: square, diagonal, two round, tripod. | Knots
- [ ] 1C-5b | Use lashings to make a camp gadget or structure. | Knots
- [ ] 1C-6a | Demonstrate how to sharpen a knife, ax, and hatchet. | Tools
- [ ] 1C-6b | Demonstrate safe handling and use of knife, ax, saw. | Tools

## First Aid & Emergency Prep
- [ ] 1C-7a | Demonstrate bandages for sprained ankle, head wound, upper arm, collarbone, hand. | First Aid
- [ ] 1C-7b | Explain: " hurry cases" plus heatstroke, dehydration, hypothermia, frostbite. | First Aid
- [ ] 1C-7c | Demonstrate how to transport an injured person alone and with a helper. | First Aid
- [ ] 1C-7d | Teach another scout a first-aid skill using the EDGE method. | First Aid
- [ ] 1C-8a | Explain what to do for a missing-person situation. | Emergency
- [ ] 1C-8b | Explain the plan for an emergency mobilization of your troop. | Emergency

## Aquatics
- [ ] 1C-9a | Swim 150 yards with rests: strong stroke, rest, side stroke, rest, elementary backstroke. | Aquatics
- [ ] 1C-9b | Explain and demonstrate: reach, throw, row, go (with support) rescues. | Aquatics
- [ ] 1C-9c | Demonstrate line rescue with a throw line. | Aquatics

## Citizenship, Service, Leadership
- [ ] 1C-10a | Participate in a troop/patrol flag ceremony. | Citizenship
- [ ] 1C-10b | Plan and carry out a service project (minimum two hours). | Service
- [ ] 1C-10c | Identify or serve in a position of responsibility; explain your duties. | Leadership
- [ ] 1C-11 | Tell how you practiced the Outdoor Code and Leave No Trace on outings. | Outdoors
- [ ] 1C-12a | Fitness retest: pushups, situps, sit-and-reach, 1-mile. Set next goals. | Fitness
- [ ] 1C-12b | Describe a healthy diet and how your eating habits compare. | Fitness
- [ ] 1C-13 | Complete a Scoutmaster conference. | Conference
- [ ] 1C-14 | Complete a board of review. | Board
